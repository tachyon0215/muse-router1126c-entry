# muse-router1126c-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Router Confesses](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-the-router-confesses-f98f6b9e).

## The entry

> In my defence, I have seen how this film ends 400 times in your cache, and I refused to watch you cry at the twist again.

Candidates (3 or more) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: it speaks in the router's own smug voice - only the router has counted the ending 400 times in the cache - and the excuse twists at the end into petty mercy: it cut the film to spare you the twist.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
